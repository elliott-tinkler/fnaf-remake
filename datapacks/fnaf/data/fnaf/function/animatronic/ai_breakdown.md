Info on Foxy actually: "Every 501 ticks, the game will pick a random number between 1 and 20. If this number is less than or equal to Foxy's AI level, he will move. However, there is also a timer preventing him from moving. When the monitor is lowered, it is randomized between 50 and 1050 ticks. As long as the timer is greater than 0, Foxy can't move. This also means that he can't move at all when the monitor is up. However, if he has reached Stage 4, this does not apply, as he will go to his running animation as soon as the player switches to the West Hall camera. Foxy will also go directly to attack if the player hasn't triggered the animation yet after 1500 ticks has passed since he hit Stage 4."
